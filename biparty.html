Congressional Bipartisanship Scores

Member-level attract and offer scores for every U.S. House and Senate member, 98th–118th Congresses (1983–2024). Two complementary measures — attract (out-party cosponsor draw) and offer (cross-party support given) — computed overall and within 34 CRS policy areas. Introduced in Dobson & Lollis (2026).

Visualization
plot_bipartisanship(congress, chambers = c("house", "senate"), highlight = NULL)
Violin plot of issue-area score distributions by party and chamber. Optionally highlight a specific member by Bioguide ID.
117th Congress, Senate, with member highlight
plot_bipartisanship(117, chambers = "senate", highlight = "P000449")
plot_trend(members, type = "member", chamber = NULL, score_type = "attract")
Time-series line chart of bipartisanship scores across Congresses. Use type = "member" to trace individual careers or type = "party" for party-average trends.
Individual careers — Portman and Klobuchar
plot_trend(c("P000449", "K000367"), score_type = "attract")
Party-average attract trend — Senate
plot_trend(c("D", "R"), type = "party", chamber = "senate", score_type = "attract")
plot_member_profile(member, congress, chamber)
Full profile card for a single member in a single Congress: photo, career summary statistics, and a bar chart of attract and offer scores across all 34 CRS policy areas. Requires the magick and patchwork packages.
Rob Portman — 117th Congress
plot_member_profile("P000449", congress = 117, chamber = "senate")
